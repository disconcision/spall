Concept renders

A kit-of-parts megastructure renderer in Blender. One JSON spec, a list of parts on a 3D grid, drives everything below: concept art in five looks, cutaways, sections and plans, lighting, a phased construction time-lapse, and palette-locked 2:1 pixel sprites. None of it was modelled by hand.

Stratum era · 30 Sep 2026 · Blender 5.0 EEVEE · rebuild with ./run_all.sh (about 2 min) · the six agent-designed buildings that came next are on their own page
kit.py is the renderer (43 part builders, materials, views); gen.py the seeded generators; agent_check.py the spec validator; sprites.py the sprite pipeline; post.py the captions and sheets.
