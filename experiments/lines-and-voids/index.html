How it works

Drag from any surface to lay a cable; it feeds out as you go and anchors on the nearest grid line where you let go. Release over nothing and the end hangs free.

Drag a cable to fling it; drag an end to re-route it. Heavier gauges lag and swing.

Tie: alt-drag (or T) a stroke across cables to cinch them into a ribbon. Two ties on the same cables make a sleeved trunk. Hang clamps a tie to the ceiling or nearest wall.

The world is a voxel grid with exact shapes carved out of it, so the shaft walls are truly round; cables are free curves, never cells.
